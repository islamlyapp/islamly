# **App Name**: Islamly

## Core Features:

- Text Library Browsing: Effortlessly browse and view a meticulously curated collection of classical Islamic texts and scholarly works aligned with the Salafi/Athari methodology.
- Advanced Text Search: Efficiently search within the comprehensive text library using keywords, authors, or topics to pinpoint specific information and references.
- Scholarly Passage Explanation Tool: Utilize a generative AI tool to provide simplified explanations or contextual details for complex scholarly passages within the curated texts, aiding deeper understanding.
- Daily Adhkar Display: Access a reliable collection of daily supplications (Adhkar) presented with clear Arabic text, transliteration, and authentic translations.
- Accurate Prayer Times: Display precise daily prayer times (Salah) based on the user's geographical location and preferred calculation method.
- Bookmark & Notes for Texts: Enable users to bookmark favorite texts or specific pages and add personal notes directly within the application for future study and quick reference.
- Quran Study: Browse and read the Quran with authentic translations and tafsir (exegesis).
- Hadith Explorer: Navigate and search through collections of authentic Hadith with commentary.
- Seerah Navigator: Explore the biography of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and key historical events.
- Islamic Quizzes: Test knowledge on various Islamic topics, including Quran, Hadith, Seerah, and Fiqh.
- Native Mobile Platform: The application is developed as a native mobile app for optimal performance and integration with device features.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A deep, dignified red (#AD1F37) to symbolize the importance of faith and authentic knowledge within the Salafi/Athari methodology.
- Background color: An almost-black (#0F0B0B) with a subtle red undertone, providing a serious and focused visual environment conducive to scholarly study.
- Accent color: A soft, luminous magenta (#E085C1) used sparingly to highlight interactive elements or crucial information, offering subtle contrast without distracting from the main content.
- Headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque sans-serif for a modern, objective, and clear presentation. Body text font: 'Literata', a transitional serif, chosen for its literary, vintage, and formal feel, ensuring excellent readability for extended scholarly texts.
- Use minimalist, outline-style icons to maintain a clean aesthetic that complements the serious and academic nature of the app, ensuring clarity without unnecessary embellishment.
- Prioritize a clean, content-focused layout with generous dark space. Implement a clear hierarchical structure for information presentation, optimizing readability and user focus on text.
- Incorporate subtle and swift animations, such as gentle fades and slides for content transitions, to enhance user experience without disrupting the focused environment.
